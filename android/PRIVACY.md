# Wolle Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-08-18

Wolle is an offline-first Android client for a self-hosted [Stricknani](../README.md) instance.
This privacy policy describes the information handled by the Wolle app (`blue.anika.wolle`).

## Information we collect

Wolle does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information to us or any third party.
It has no analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting service of its own.

The only server Wolle talks to is the Stricknani server URL you configure yourself. Your
server's base URL and the personal access token you generate in Stricknani's own Settings page
are stored locally on your device, encrypted at rest. Wolle never asks for or stores your
Stricknani account password. Projects, yarn stash entries, and their photos fetched from your
Stricknani server are cached locally on your device so the app works offline; that data never
leaves your device except in requests to your own configured server.

Removing the app or clearing its application data removes all locally stored information
according to Android's normal behavior.

## External links

The Settings screen may link to the Wolle GitHub repository and this privacy policy in an
external browser. Those services may collect information under their own privacy policies; Wolle
does not control or receive that information.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app's behavior changes. The current version is always
available in the Stricknani repository. Questions or privacy concerns can be reported through the
[GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/pschmitt/stricknani/issues).
