Stricknani is an offline-first Android client for your own self-hosted Stricknani server.

Connect the app to any Stricknani instance with its server URL and a personal access token. Your
account password is never stored in the app. Once connected, projects and yarn stash entries stay
available on your device when you are offline and synchronize with your configured server when a
connection is available.

Features:
• Browse and search knitting projects and yarn stash entries
• View descriptions, notes, steps, tags, photos, and linked yarns
• Create and edit projects and yarn entries, including while offline
• Keep changes in a local queue until the configured server is reachable
• Use the gauge calculator without a network connection
• Choose light, dark, or system appearance and configure the navigation bar

Stricknani does not provide a hosted account or server. You choose the Stricknani server that the
app connects to and remain responsible for that server, its data, and its privacy policy. The app
contains no advertising or analytics service.
